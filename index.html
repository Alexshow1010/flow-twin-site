Flow Twin

Flow Twin is a daily luck rhythm and Living Crystal card app for iOS and iPadOS. Sense your energy, intent, and resistance in 5 seconds to reveal your daily Luck Tier and twin companion guidance on your device.

Highlights

  • 3 free daily draws (resets at 04:00 local Flow Day).
